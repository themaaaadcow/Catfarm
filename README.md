CATFARM

Catfarm is an interactive idle desktop game. As you work on your computer, your little cat will farm and gain money to buy more types of crops, larger crop beds, better soil, etc. 

The cat's output can be accelerated by doing certain actions. Clicking will cause crops to grow slightly faster for a very short period of time, typing on your keyboard will accelerate your cats movement, etc. 

Currency can be gained by selling crops to buyers. This currency can be used to buy upgrades to your farm, or it can be used for cosmetics - decorations for your screen, your farm, your cat's house or Cathome (more on that in a bit). 

Multiple screens - This isn't just an idle farming game - Your cat can also do other things idly too! Going to the gym is one example. Tapping the right and left arrow keys on the number pad (provided that numlock is off) can switch screens - The center screen is the farm, left is Cathome, and right is the gym. 

Gym - Kitty wants to pump some iron! Similarly to the farm, your kitty will idly go from one machine to the next on this screen - and her workout can be boosted by doing things like clicking and typing. (Note - As kitty gets stronger, her performance on the farm will get better too!)

Cathome - Kitty can't be working out or farming ALL the time - give her some rest by visiting Cathome once in a while! You can purchase decorations from the shop to put here, customize kitty's area, etc. 

Cosmetics - Kitty wants to look her best! Wearable cosmetics such as hats, glasses, necklaces, and many more are also available in the shop.
